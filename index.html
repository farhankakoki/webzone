Founder & Vision

Trusted Digital Service Hub

Webzone was established by Azharudheen Velleri with a clear mission: to simplify digital services for everyone. Our goal is to remove the complexity of online applications and make essential services accessible through technology you use every day.

Inspired by the CSC (Common Service Center) model, we prioritize trust and reliability in every interaction. We believe that a customer-first approach is the key to creating a truly helpful digital gateway for the community.
